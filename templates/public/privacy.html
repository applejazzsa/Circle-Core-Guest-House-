Privacy Policy

Circle Core Guest House stores accommodation operations data for each customer workspace, including guest, booking, room, payment, housekeeping, and report records entered by authorised users.

Customer workspace data is separated by tenant workspace. Circle Core staff may access a workspace only for support, billing, security, or operational assistance.

Personal information must be used only for lawful accommodation operations, support, billing, and required record keeping. Customers remain responsible for ensuring the information they enter is accurate and collected with proper authority.

Data access, correction, export, and deletion requests can be submitted through the data request process.
