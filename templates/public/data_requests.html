Export, Correction, and Deletion Requests

Workspace owners can export operational records from the reports area where export is available on their plan.

Guest access, correction, and deletion requests should be sent to the guest house that collected the information. Circle Core can assist the workspace owner with export or deletion where legally permitted.

Deletion requests may be declined or delayed where records must be retained for accounting, tax, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or legal obligations.

Support requests should include the guest house name, requester name, contact details, and the specific record or date range involved.
