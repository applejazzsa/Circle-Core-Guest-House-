Questions, answered

Is there a setup fee? +

No. There are no setup fees. Your 30-day trial starts immediately.

What happens when my trial ends? +

You will be prompted to select a plan. Your data stays in place, and the team can help you renew before expiry.

Can I change plans later? +

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade as your property changes, and billing can be adjusted from the next cycle.

How do I pay? +

We invoice you for EFT payment. Card payments can be added later when the payment gateway is ready.
